ENTERPRISE COMPLIANCE

Trust Center — Enterprise-Grade Security, Privacy & GDPR Compliance

Local-First & Air-Gapped by default (with optional BYOC Cloud connection). Strict governance, deterministic execution, and sovereign control over corporate financial data.

🔒 Q1: Does ZUKWEND upload or store financial data in the cloud?

A: No. ZUKWEND is Local-First & Air-Gapped by default (with optional BYOC Cloud connection). In ZUKWEND CORE AI mode, invoice data never leaves company hardware. Zero cloud storage, zero external data transmission, zero model training on proprietary documents.

⚖️ Q2: How does ZUKWEND ensure GDPR Art. 25 & Art. 44 compliance?

A: By processing all invoice PDFs, metadata, and aging evaluations entirely on local premises, ZUKWEND guarantees native Privacy-by-Design (Art. 25) and eliminates cross-border extra-EU data transfers (Art. 44). Full compliance for legal auditors, IT directors, and Data Protection Officers (DPO).

⚙️ Q3: What is the 3-Tier AI Architecture and who controls execution?

A: Absolute enterprise administrator control across 3 isolated tiers: Built-in Native Offline Engine (1s, 100% air-gapped), Local LLM via Ollama (localhost), or BYOC (Bring Your Own Key) connecting directly to your corporate OpenAI, Claude, or Gemini accounts.

🛡️ Q4: Is the executable binary verified and digitally signed?

A: Yes. The Windows desktop binary is cryptographically signed with an Authenticode Extended Validation (EV) certificate, ensuring maximum Microsoft Defender SmartScreen reputation, tamper-proof binary integrity, and zero antivirus false positives.

👤 Q5: Can the AI send emails or legal notices autonomously without oversight?

A: No. ZUKWEND operates strictly under Human-in-the-Loop governance. No irreversible actions occur automatically: every aging classification, deduction score, formal notice, and export (PDF, Excel, JSON) is fully reviewable and requires human verification prior to dispatch.

📁 Q6: How does ZUKWEND handle clients with multiple overdue invoices simultaneously?

A: ZUKWEND automatically consolidates open items for the same debtor via strict 100% Tax ID / VAT verification, creating a single, permanent client dossier under output/CLIENTS/{CLIENT_VAT}/{DATE}/. It generates a single Consolidated Statement of Account (STATEMENT_OF_ACCOUNT_CONSOLIDATED.pdf) with itemized breakdown and currency-partitioned subtotals (e.g. EUR, USD), alongside a single unified email notice (REMINDER_EMAIL_CONSOLIDATED.txt), eliminating duplicate and fragmented reminders. Individual invoices are neatly archived in the Individual_Invoices_Archive/ subfolder.
